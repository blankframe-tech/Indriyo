# Indriyo (ইন্দ্রিয়) - Brand & Identity Guidelines

## 🌟 Brand Vision & Ethos
**"The Sixth Sense for Motorcyclists"**

*Indriyo* (from Sanskrit / Bengali for "Sense" or "Organ of Perception") represents artificial situational intuition. It bridges the gap between mechanical machines and human biological perception, giving riders eyes where physical optics fail.

---

## 🔤 Typography & Logos

- **English Logotype:** All-caps bold geometric sans-serif (e.g., Space Grotesk, Montserrat, SF Pro Display).
- **Bengali Sub-mark:** **ইন্দ্রিয়** in clean modern Bengali typography (e.g., Kalpurush or Noto Sans Bengali).
- **HUD Readouts & Telemetry:** Monospaced high-legibility font (SF Mono, Fira Code, JetBrains Mono).

---

## 📢 Brand Voice & Messaging
- **Technical yet approachable:** Grounded in real physics (Optical Tau, mmWave radar, 30 FPS inference) rather than vaporware marketing.
- **Rider-first empathy:** Focused on the real hazards of urban riding (Dhaka gridlock, chaotic lane splits, sudden highway tailgating).
- **Frugal engineering pride:** Emphasizes how intelligent software and clever engineering can provide automotive-grade ADAS safety for under ৳3,000 BDT.
