The Indriyo Architecture

Bridging the white space between screenless radar and dumb display dashcams.

👁️

Active Blind Spot Detection (BSD)

Seamlessly monitors vehicles lingering in 120°–165° left and right rear quadrants. Triggers an immediate solid amber peripheral border so you know a vehicle is there without breaking head posture.

⚡

Predictive Time-to-Collision (TTC)

Uses Optical Expansion (Tau Theory) and relative velocity vectoring. Differentiates between a car pacing safely behind and a high-speed vehicle on a collision course (< 1.8s TTC).

🧭

Context-Adaptive Telemetry

Connects to your bike speed via OBD-II, GPS, or ThrottleIQ middleware. Scales sensitivity dynamically: stopped at a red light, approaching traffic triggers alerts from further away!

🚨

Intuitive Peripheral UX

The human peripheral eye is highly sensitive to color strobes. Solid Amber draws gentle attention; rapidly flashing Crimson Red instantly triggers evasive action before a rear collision occurs.

🏍️

Stealth Tail Pod Design

No bulky 360° dome cameras or awkward radar boxes. Dual waterproof miniature optical lenses tuck invisibly beneath the pillion seat or license plate bracket.

📱

ThrottleIQ Smartphone Bridge

Zero extra compute cost! Use your mounted phone as the AI brain via our drop-in Flutter module. Your phone ingests the ESP32 Wi-Fi feed, runs YOLO inference, and provides helmet audio warnings.

Electrical Engineering

Motorcycle Safe Wiring Schematic

Protected from alternator voltage spikes, engine vibration, and battery parasitic drain.

[ Motorcycle Switched 12V Line (License Plate / Tail Light) ] │ ├───(+) Red Wire ──► [ 3A Inline Fuse ] ──► IN+ [ LM2596 Buck ] OUT+ ──┬──► (+) [1000µF Cap] ──► 5.1V [Left ESP32-CAM] │ │ │ └──► (+) [1000µF Cap] ──► 5.1V [Right ESP32-CAM] │ └───(-) Black Wire ───────────────────────► IN- [ Converter ] OUT- ──┬──► (-) [1000µF Cap] ──► GND [Left ESP32-CAM] │ └──► (-) [1000µF Cap] ──► GND [Right ESP32-CAM]
⚡

1. Calibrate Potentiometer First

Factory buck converters often ship set to 10V–12V. Turn the brass adjustment screw counter-clockwise until your multimeter reads exactly 5.1V before plugging in the ESP32s!

🛡️

2. Anti-Brownout Capacitors

Solder one 1000µF 16V electrolytic capacitor directly across the 5V and GND pins of each ESP32 board to absorb Wi-Fi transmission spikes and stop reboots.

🔒

3. Vibration Locking

Motorcycle engine RPM vibrations can drift adjustment screws. Add a drop of clear nail polish or silicone adhesive to lock the screw head permanently in place.
