# Indriyo Hardware Mounting & Calibration Guide

Proper physical mounting of the dual tail cameras is essential for accurate distance and Time-To-Collision (TTC) calculations.

---

## 📍 Ideal Mounting Positions

1. **Under the Tail Pillion:**
   Tucked directly beneath the passenger seat or tail tidy light bracket. Keeps the lenses shielded from road spray and gravel.
2. **Above the Rear License Plate:**
   Mounted to the license plate bolt holes using the M4 screw channels integrated into the 3D printed housing.

---

## 📐 Alignment Angles

| Parameter | Recommended Value | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Mounting Height** | 75 cm – 95 cm from road surface | Standard motorcycle tail height |
| **Tilt Angle** | 3° to 5° downwards | Focuses the sensor on road traffic rather than open sky |
| **Outward Splay** | 15° each side (Left and Right) | Extends lateral blind-spot coverage to 170° |

---

## 🌧️ Weatherproofing Checklist

- Print in **PETG** or **ABS** (do NOT use PLA, as engine exhaust heat and summer sunlight will deform PLA).
- Coat the camera seams with a thin bead of silicone sealant or RTV black silicone.
- Ensure the lens hood faces slightly downward so rain droplets run off rather than accumulating on the lens glass.
- Use rubber O-rings or neoprene washers under mounting screws to isolate the cameras from high-RPM engine vibration.
